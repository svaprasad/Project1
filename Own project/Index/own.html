Farmer’s Daily Activities

From sunrise to sunset – a farmer’s hard-working day

🌅 Morning

Field Preparation

Farmers start their day early in the morning. They prepare the fields, plough the land, and check soil conditions. Morning time is best for heavy work due to cool weather.

☀️ Afternoon

Irrigation & Crop Care

During afternoon, farmers irrigate crops, remove weeds, and protect plants from pests. Proper water management is very important at this stage.

🌇 Evening

Harvesting & Planning

In the evening, farmers harvest crops, store produce, and plan for the next day. They also take care of animals and tools before resting.
